# DigitalX Solutions Backlink Kit

Backlinks are earned off site. This is the action plan and the copy to use.
Work top to bottom. Use the same name, one line description, and links
everywhere (see BRAND_ENTITY_KIT.md) so each link also reinforces the entity.

Reuse this one liner in every profile and pitch:

DigitalX Solutions is a web development, AI automation, and performance marketing agency serving service businesses in the United States and Australia.

Avoid: paid link schemes, private blog networks, bulk fiverr links, and spammy
directories. These trigger Google penalties and are not worth it. Aim for
relevant, real, editorial or profile links.

---

## Tier 1: Agency directories and review platforms (do first)

These are where buyers and AI assistants look for best agency lists. High value.

- Clutch (clutch.co) create a profile, gather client reviews
- G2 (g2.com) list the agency and services
- DesignRush (designrush.com)
- GoodFirms (goodfirms.co)
- The Manifest (themanifest.com)
- Sortlist (sortlist.com)
- UpCity (upcity.com)
- Trustpilot (trustpilot.com) for reviews and a brand link

Action: claim or create each profile, paste the standard description, add the
website link and the same social links, then ask 3 to 5 happy clients to leave
a review. Reviews plus a link is the strongest combination.

---

## Tier 2: Tool partner directories (high relevance, often missed)

You use these tools, so a listing is legitimate and very relevant. Relevance
matters more than volume.

- GoHighLevel: agency and expert directory or marketplace listing
- n8n: experts, partners, or creators listing (n8n.io)
- Vapi: partners or agencies page if available
- Twilio: partner or build directory
- Meta Business Partners: apply if you qualify on ad spend managed
- Shopify Partners: list if you build Shopify or ecommerce work
- Vercel: showcase or partners if you publish a strong build

Action: search each tool site for partner, agency, expert, or directory and
apply. These pages carry strong topical relevance for your services.

---

## Tier 3: Business and local citations

Consistent name, address, and phone across these builds trust and entity signal.

- Google Business Profile (set up as a service area business, hide address)
- Bing Places for Business
- Crunchbase
- LinkedIn company page
- Australia: Oneflare, True Local, Yellow Pages AU, Hotfrog, StartLocal, Aussie Web
- United States: Yelp, Better Business Bureau if registered, local chambers

Action: keep the name and description identical to the kit on every one.

---

## Tier 4: Digital PR and guest posts (editorial links, highest authority)

- HARO style services: Connectively, Featured, Help A B2B Writer, Qwoted.
  Respond to journalist questions about automation, AI, small business, and
  marketing. A quote earns a link from a real publication.
- Guest posts: pitch marketing and small business blogs that accept
  contributors. Topics you can own: AI receptionists, missed call recovery,
  GoHighLevel, n8n vs Zapier, server side tracking.
- Podcasts: appear on small business, trades, or marketing podcasts. Show notes
  usually link back.
- Partnerships: co marketing with complementary providers (web hosts, booking
  tools, local trade associations) that link to partners.

---

## Tier 5: Communities and a product launch

- Reddit: answer real questions in r slash smallbusiness, r slash agency,
  r slash gohighlevel, r slash automation. AI assistants cite Reddit heavily.
  Be genuinely helpful, link only when it truly helps.
- Quora: answer questions about AI receptionists, automation, and agencies.
- Indie Hackers and small business forums.
- Product Hunt: launch the free voice agent demo as a product. A launch earns
  links and traffic and is a natural fit.

## Outreach templates

### Guest post pitch

Subject: Guest article idea for [site name]

Hi [name],

I write about automation and lead capture for service businesses and I read
[site name] regularly. I would like to contribute a practical, non promotional
article for your readers. A few angles that fit your audience:

1. How AI receptionists recover missed call revenue for local businesses
2. n8n vs Zapier for real business automation
3. Why server side tracking is now mandatory for ad measurement

I run DigitalX Solutions, a web development and automation agency in the USA
and Australia, so the examples come from real builds. Happy to write to your
guidelines and word count. Would any of these be a fit?

Thanks,
[your name], DigitalX Solutions, digitalx-solutions.com

### Partnership or co marketing

Subject: Partner idea between [their company] and DigitalX Solutions

Hi [name],

We build websites and automation for service businesses and our clients often
need [their service]. I think our audiences overlap without competing. Would
you be open to listing each other as recommended partners, or doing a simple
content swap that links back to both sites?

Thanks,
[your name], DigitalX Solutions

### Journalist or expert quote (HARO style)

Hi [name],

Quick answer to your question on [topic]:

[Two to three sentences of genuinely useful insight, no fluff.]

You can attribute this to [your name], founder of DigitalX Solutions, a web
development and automation agency serving the USA and Australia
(digitalx-solutions.com). Happy to expand if useful.
