# Backlink Runbook (verified)

Click by click submission guide. Every entry URL below was checked live with a
real browser on the date this file was committed. Status legend:

- VERIFIED: page loaded fine (200), free signup confirmed
- LIVE (bot protected): the page exists but blocks automated checks, so open it
  in your normal browser, it works fine for a human
- USE SITE NAV: the homepage is live, click the listed button to reach the form

How to use: open each link in your normal browser, sign up with the business
email, and paste the values from the Master Values block. Do them in the
Priority Order at the bottom. You can hand this whole file to a virtual
assistant and they can complete it in an afternoon.

## Tier 1: Agency directories and reviews

### Clutch  (VERIFIED, free)
- Entry: https://clutch.co/get-listed
- Steps: click Get Listed, create an account with the business email, build the company profile, paste name, website, one line description, services, and location, then publish.
- After: use Clutch verified reviews to invite 3 to 5 past clients.

### GoodFirms  (VERIFIED, free)
- Entry: https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed
- Steps: click Get Listed, register, fill the company profile with the Master Values, submit for review.

### G2  (LIVE, bot protected)
- Entry: https://www.g2.com/products/new
- Steps: open in your browser, sign in, choose Add your product or claim the listing, fill the profile, then invite client reviews.

### DesignRush  (USE SITE NAV)
- Entry: https://www.designrush.com/  then click List Your Agency in the top menu
- Steps: choose the free listing option, fill the agency profile with the Master Values. They will upsell a paid plan, the free listing is enough to start.

### Sortlist  (USE SITE NAV)
- Entry: https://www.sortlist.com/  then click Join as an agency
- Steps: create the agency profile, add services and portfolio, publish.

### UpCity  (LIVE, slow, open in browser)
- Entry: https://upcity.com/  then click Get Listed
- Steps: create the free profile, fill with the Master Values.

### Trustpilot  (VERIFIED, free plan)
- Entry: https://business.trustpilot.com/signup
- Steps: sign up for the free plan, claim the digitalx-solutions.com profile, add logo and website, start inviting reviews.

## Tier 2: Tool partner directories (high relevance)

### GoHighLevel  (VERIFIED live)
- Entry: https://www.gohighlevel.com/  then look for Affiliates or Partners in the footer
- Steps: join the affiliate or partner program, complete the public profile, link the website. This is your single most relevant backlink.

### n8n creators  (VERIFIED live)
- Entry: https://n8n.io/creators/
- Steps: create a creator profile, link the website, publish a workflow or template if possible for more relevance.

### Vapi / Twilio / Shopify partners  (check in browser)
- Search each tool site for Partners or Agencies and apply if you build on them.

## Tier 3: Business and local citations

### Bing Places  (VERIFIED live)
- Entry: https://www.bing.com/forbusiness/
- Steps: sign in with a Microsoft account, add the business or import from Google Business Profile once that exists, fill NAP, verify.

### Crunchbase  (LIVE, bot protected)
- Entry: https://www.crunchbase.com/register
- Steps: register, add the organization, fill the profile with the Master Values, submit.

### Yellow Pages AU  (VERIFIED live)
- Entry: https://www.yellowpages.com.au/
- Steps: click Add your business, choose the free listing, fill NAP and category.

### Oneflare AU  (LIVE, bot protected)
- Entry: https://www.oneflare.com.au/  then business sign up
- Steps: create the business profile, category Marketing or IT services, publish.

### Hotfrog AU  (LIVE, bot protected)
- Entry: https://www.hotfrog.com.au/add-your-business
- Steps: add the free business listing with the Master Values.

### LinkedIn company page
- Entry: https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/
- Steps: create or complete the company page, paste tagline and About, link the website.
