# Antigravity SEO Blueprint: digitalx-solutions.com

Prepared for Jazib, DigitalX Solutions. Scope: AI Voice Receptionist, Missed-Call Text-Back and GoHighLevel CRM, serving trades, medical and SMEs in Australia and the US.

## Read this first: strategy and assumptions

The reality of the SERP. Head terms like "AI voice agent" and "AI receptionist" are owned by funded platforms (Bland, Retell, Vapi, Synthflow, Smith.ai, Goodcall) with thousands of referring domains. A new agency site will not outrank them head-on in 2026. The Antigravity play is to stack three narrow advantages they can't match: (1) industry + city specificity, (2) integration-specific how-to content (GoHighLevel, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Cliniko), and (3) real operator proof (case studies, founder story, verified AU office). Once the long-tail clusters rank and earn links, the head terms get reachable.

"AI voice SEO" covers two goals, and this plan handles both: ranking in Google for AI voice agent keywords (Steps 1-6), and getting cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews (see the AEO section at the end).

Assumptions I made so you can implement immediately:

- Office: Casula NSW. Anything I can't know (street address, phone, coordinates, social URLs) is marked [IN BRACKETS]. Replace these before publishing; wrong NAP data hurts local trust.
- Competition ratings are relative estimates, not tool data. Validate volume and KD in Ahrefs, Semrush or Google Search Console before you commit writing time. Start with the Low-rated rows.
- I avoided unprovable claims in copy (e.g., specific latency or uptime numbers). Insert real numbers from your own system where I've flagged [YOUR NUMBER]. Accurate claims also protect you from ad and consumer-law issues.
- AU spelling is used for Australian pages (enquiries, diaries). Use US spelling on any US-targeted variants.

Rules that keep topical authority tight:

- Max 3 clicks from the homepage to any page; every pillar is linked from the main nav.
- City pages must be genuinely different (local call-volume context, local regulations, a real job example). Thin city-swapped duplicates get filtered. Build only the cities you can write real content for.
- Breadcrumbs on every page with BreadcrumbList schema.
- One primary keyword per URL. If two pages chase the same query, merge them.
### Internal linking: passing authority from /insights/ to /solutions/

Anchor text mix: roughly 50% partial match, 25% exact match, 25% natural or branded. Place the first link within the first 150 words, use contextual body links (not footer or sidebar), and add a mid-article link plus a closing CTA link. Every money page links back to its 3-5 best supporting insights, and spokes link to sibling spokes.

## STEP 6: Backlink and E-E-A-T sprint

### Three guest post and digital PR angles

- For trade platforms and associations (original data play): "The After-Hours Answer Rate Study." Mystery-call 50-100 plumbing, electrical and HVAC businesses in Sydney, Melbourne and a US metro after 6pm. Record the answer rate, time to call-back and what happened. Publish the dataset on /insights/ and pitch the findings to trade publications, associations and local business press. Original data is the single most reliable link earner in this niche.
- For SaaS and automation communities (technical teardown): "How we wired an AI voice agent into GoHighLevel with n8n." Architecture diagram, failure modes, latency tuning and lessons learned. Pitch to HighLevel agency communities, automation blogs and no-code publications. Technical credibility draws links from other builders.
- For medical and dental platforms (compliance-first): "What a missed new-patient call really costs a clinic, and how to use AI call answering responsibly." Cover the revenue maths, patient privacy, consent and recording disclosures. Pitch to practice-management blogs and professional-association newsletters. Have a qualified person review any legal or privacy statements before publishing.
Pitch mechanics: send a 4-line email (hook, the data, why their audience cares, offer of exclusive access), personalise to one recent piece of theirs, and follow up once after 5 days. Aim for 20-30 targeted pitches per angle rather than mass outreach.

### Leveraging partners (GoHighLevel, ServiceTitan, Twilio)

Partner links are earned through real participation, not asked for cold. Practical route:

- GoHighLevel: publish your integration tutorials, contribute in the official community and agency groups, and apply for any certified-expert or partner directory listing that links to your site. Share your GHL case study with their community team.
- ServiceTitan and Jobber: list yourself in their marketplace or partner and consultant directories where available, and write an integration guide they can reference from their help or community pages.
- Twilio: check the partner and ISV programmes, then submit a joint use case. Twilio regularly features builder case studies.
- Co-marketing: offer a co-hosted webinar ("Never miss a call-out: AI call answering for ServiceTitan users"), a joint case study or a shared checklist asset. Each should include a link back to the relevant /integrations/ page.
- Quick directories (same sprint): Google Business Profile, Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, plus AU directories such as True Local, Hotfrog and Yellow Pages Australia. Keep NAP identical everywhere.
- Journalist-request platforms: register on Qwoted or Featured and respond to AI, automation and small-business queries weekly.
### On-page E-E-A-T setup

- Founder story page (/about/): real name, photo, background, why you built DigitalX, and your actual experience with GoHighLevel and AI voice systems. Add Person schema and link to LinkedIn. Be honest about your setup (for example, a Sydney office with a distributed team) since inconsistencies erode trust.
- Case study workflow: (1) get written client permission, (2) record baseline metrics (answer rate, missed calls, bookings) before launch, (3) record the same metrics 30 and 60 days after, (4) publish problem, setup, outcome, and a client quote with their name and business, (5) add Review or testimonial markup only for real reviews shown on the page.
- Verified location signals: Google Business Profile verified, address and phone identical on every page footer, the contact page and schema, an embedded map, and local directory citations.
- Author attribution on /insights/: every article gets a named author, short bio, publish and updated dates, and cited sources. Medical or legal compliance content gets a reviewer line.
- Trust pages: privacy policy, terms, security and data-handling page, call-recording and consent explainer, and an editorial standards note.
- Proof assets: demo call recordings, a short walkthrough video, and a public integrations list.

### AEO: getting cited by AI answer engines and voice search

- Write definition-style answers in the first lines under question headings ("What is an AI voice receptionist?" followed by a 40-60 word answer).
- Keep entity facts consistent everywhere: company name, services, location, founder, service areas.
- Publish original data and clear comparison tables; AI engines favour citable, specific sources.
- Earn mentions on third-party sites (directories, partner pages, industry roundups), because engines weigh what others say about you.
