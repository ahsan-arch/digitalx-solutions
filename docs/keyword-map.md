## Summary of Cannibalization Controls
1. **Pillars vs. Integrations**: `/solutions/ai-voice-receptionists` targets the service level (*"AI voice receptionist"*), while `/integrations/gohighlevel` targets technical execution (*"GoHighLevel AI voice agent"*).
2. **Pillars vs. Verticals**: `/solutions/missed-call-text-back` targets the technology category (*"missed call text back"*), while `/industries/plumbers-hvac-trades` targets the vertical application (*"AI receptionist for plumbers"*).
3. **Pillars vs. Insights**: `/solutions/ai-agents-chatbots` targets buyers seeking implementation (*"AI agents for business"*), while `/insights/what-is-an-ai-agent` targets research queries (*"what is an AI agent"*).
