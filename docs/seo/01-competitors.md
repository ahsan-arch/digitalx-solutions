## 1. Cluster A: AI Receptionist / Voice Agent

### Target Queries:
- `AI receptionist for plumbers` / `AI receptionist for dental clinics`
- `AI voice agent for home services`
- `AI answering service vs human receptionist`
- `AI receptionist cost 2026` / `AI receptionist Australia`

### Top Organic Competitors Analyzed:
1. **Bland.ai / Vapi Marketplace Solutions** (Global/US)
2. **Smith.ai** (US — Hybrid AI + Human answering service)
3. **Phonely.ai / Dialzara** (US — Productized AI phone agent)
4. **Curacall / MyReceptionist** (AU — Healthcare & Dental answering)
5. **TradieCore / ServiceM8 Partner Ecosystems** (AU)

### Competitor Structural Patterns:
* **Titles**: Keyword-first with industry identifier:
  `AI Receptionist for Plumbers: 24/7 After-Hours Answering | [Brand]` (< 58 chars).
* **Headings (H1–H3)**:
  - H1: Exact trade + outcome: *“Never Miss an Emergency Plumbing Call Again”*.
  - H2s: Trade-specific call scenarios (Burst pipes, water heater failure, weekend pricing quotes).
  - H2: *“How It Integrates with ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro”* (or ServiceM8 in AU).
  - H2: *“Cost Comparison: AI Voice Agent vs In-House Receptionist vs Call Center”*.
  - H2: *“Listen to Live Sample Calls”* (Embedded MP3 audio widget).
  - H2: *“Frequently Asked Questions”* (6–8 accordions).
* **Word Count**: 1,400 – 2,100 words.
* **Schema Types**: `Service`, `FAQPage`, `SoftwareApplication`, `AudioObject` (for call recordings), `BreadcrumbList`.
* **Proof Elements**: Audio recordings of actual synthetic vs human calls, ROI calculator (missed calls x average job ticket = recovered revenue), named client quotes.
* **Primary CTAs**: "Listen to Live Demo", "Test Call Our Demo Line: (XXX) XXX-XXXX", "Book Setup Consultation".

### Competitor Structural Patterns:
* **Key Finding**: Local competitor pages rank **only** when they offer genuine local context (local area codes, time zone responsiveness, compliance with state licensing laws, local case studies). Generic doorway pages created by changing only the city name are ignored or penalized.
* **Proof Elements**: Local address in Schema (`PostalAddress`), Google Business Profile integration, Australian/US phone numbers, local client testimonials.
