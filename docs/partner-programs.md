# Partner & Certification Programs Analysis (`/docs/partner-programs.md`)

> **Governing Policy**: Only display a platform logo as "Partner" or "Certified" if DigitalX Solutions holds verified proof on file. All other platforms must be strictly labelled as **"Integrates with"** or **"Built with"** to maintain Google E-E-A-T integrity, brand compliance, and Australian Consumer Law truth in advertising standards.

---

## 1. Stated Held Credentials (Owner Verification Required)

### GoHighLevel (HighLevel)
* **Program Name**: GoHighLevel Certified Agency Partner / HighLevel Agency Affiliate
* **Requirements**: Active GHL Agency Pro subscription ($497/mo), completion of official certification exam or snapshot deployment track, and client retention benchmarks.
* **Cost**: $0 certification fee; requires active HighLevel Agency Pro plan.
* **DigitalX Qualification Status**: **Qualified**. DigitalX builds production client sub-accounts and custom snapshots.
* **Proof Requirement**: Link to official HighLevel Certified Directory profile or digital certificate badge.
* **Official Link**: [gohighlevel.com/partner-program](https://www.gohighlevel.com/)

### n8n
* **Program Name**: n8n Partner Program & Creator Community
* **Requirements**: Public n8n creator profile, published reusable workflow templates or production client integration case studies, and agreement to n8n Fair-Code partnership guidelines.
* **Cost**: Free to join Creator program; official Partner directory requires verified client deployments.
* **DigitalX Qualification Status**: **Qualified**. DigitalX builds and deploys custom Docker-based n8n automation workflows.
* **Proof Requirement**: Link to official n8n Creator profile (`https://n8n.io/creators/[username]`).
* **Official Link**: [n8n.io/creators](https://n8n.io/creators/) | [n8n.io/partners](https://n8n.io/partners)

### HubSpot
* **Program Name**: HubSpot Solutions Partner Program / HubSpot Academy Certifications
* **Requirements**: Completion of HubSpot Academy certifications (Inbound, HubSpot Marketing Software, HubSpot RevOps/Workflows) and deal registration / client portal management.
* **Cost**: Academy certifications are 100% free; Solutions Partner tiering requires software spend or active client seats.
* **DigitalX Qualification Status**: **Qualified** for Certified Individual / Solutions Partner track.
* **Proof Requirement**: Link to official HubSpot Academy digital verification URL or partner listing.
* **Official Link**: [hubspot.com/partners/solutions](https://www.hubspot.com/partners/solutions)

## 2. Prospective Partner & Integration Platforms

### 1. Twilio
* **Program Name**: Twilio Partner Program (Twilio Build) — Consulting & Technology Partner Tracks
* **Requirements**: Registration on Twilio Build portal, completion of foundational sales & developer training, and demonstrated implementation capability in Twilio Voice, SMS, or Segment CDP.
* **Cost**: Free entry tier (Registered Partner); Bronze/Silver tiers require client deal registrations.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Yes (Registered Partner level)**. DigitalX configures Twilio SIP trunking, number porting, and call forwarding for AI voice agents.
* **Display Label**: **"Built with / API Telephony Provider"** (until official certification completed).
* **Application Link**: [partners.twilio.com/s/login/SelfRegister](https://partners.twilio.com/s/login/SelfRegister)

### 2. ServiceTitan
* **Program Name**: ServiceTitan Certified Provider (STCP) & App Marketplace Partner Program
* **Requirements**: Proven track record serving trade and home-service contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical), formal application, staff training, and compliance testing.
* **Cost**: Free application; enterprise co-marketing tiers available.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Pending Track Record Review**. Best applied for after documenting 3–5 active client deployments integrating ServiceTitan dispatch APIs.
* **Display Label**: **"Integrates with / Field Services CRM"**.
* **Application Link**: [servicetitan.com/partners](https://www.servicetitan.com/partners)

### 3. Jobber
* **Program Name**: Jobber Partner Program (Agency & Technology Tracks on PartnerStack)
* **Requirements**: Marketing agency or technology consultant serving home service businesses (landscaping, cleaning, plumbing, HVAC); client referral and integration commitment.
* **Cost**: Free to join via PartnerStack.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Yes (Immediate Qualification)**. DigitalX provides digital growth and CRM booking systems directly to Jobber's core target demographics.
* **Display Label**: **"Integrates with / Home Services CRM"**.
* **Application Link**: [home.partnerstack.com/jobber](https://home.partnerstack.com/jobber)

### 4. Zapier
* **Program Name**: Zapier Certified Experts & Solutions Partner Program
* **Requirements**: An active agency business website, demonstrated workflow automation track record, completion of Zapier Expert certification modules, and 3+ verified client examples.
* **Cost**: Free.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Yes**. DigitalX qualifies for Zapier Expert certification to handle legacy client migrations to n8n or GHL.
* **Display Label**: **"Integrates with / Migration Partner"**.
* **Application Link**: [zapier.com/partners](https://zapier.com/partners)

### 5. Make (formerly Integromat)
* **Program Name**: Make Partner Program (Solution Partner Track)
* **Requirements**: Completion of Make Academy certification levels (Basics, Intermediate, Advanced) and demonstration of client integration architecture.
* **Cost**: Free for registered partners.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Yes**. Make certifications can be acquired within 1–2 business weeks.
* **Display Label**: **"Integrates with / Automation Engine"**.
* **Application Link**: [make.com/en/partners](https://www.make.com/en/partners)

### 6. Google Workspace
* **Program Name**: Google Cloud Partner Advantage Program
* **Requirements**: Company profile submission, Google Cloud/Workspace deployment capability, and credentialed team members.
* **Cost**: $0 program fee for Member level.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Yes (Member tier)**.
* **Display Label**: **"Integrates with / Google Calendar & Workspace"**.
* **Application Link**: [cloud.google.com/partners](https://cloud.google.com/partners)

### 7. Meta (Meta Business Partner)
* **Program Name**: Meta Business Partner (Agency Track)
* **Requirements**: Managed client ad spend thresholds across trailing 180 days in Meta Business Manager, active Meta Blueprint Certified individuals on staff, and strict policy compliance.
* **Cost**: Free, but conditioned on substantial managed ad spend ($10k–$50k+ quarterly depending on region).
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Subject to Managed Spend Audit**. If DigitalX manages client ad budgets directly through its Business Manager, it can apply once minimum spend thresholds are met.
* **Display Label**: **"Built with / Conversions API & Meta Ads"**.
* **Application Link**: [business.facebook.com/partners](https://business.facebook.com/partners)

### 8. OpenAI
* **Program Name**: OpenAI Partner Program / OpenAI Services Partner (Early Access & Enterprise)
* **Requirements**: Typically reserved for enterprise system integrators building directly on OpenAI APIs with significant API token consumption or custom fine-tuning architectures.
* **Cost**: Usage-based API costs.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Technology Integrator (Non-Badged)**. DigitalX builds on OpenAI GPT-4o models via API but cannot display an official partner badge without enterprise sponsorship.
* **Display Label**: **"Built with / Core LLM Intelligence"**.
* **Application Link**: [openai.com/partners](https://openai.com/)

### 9. ElevenLabs
* **Program Name**: ElevenLabs Commercial Partner Program & Creator Partner Program
* **Requirements**: Consultancies and agencies building conversational voice agents or modernizing audio infrastructure using ElevenLabs neural voice synthesis APIs.
* **Cost**: Free to apply.
* **Does DigitalX Qualify?**: **Yes (Commercial Agency Partner)**. DigitalX actively builds and demonstrates conversational AI voice agents with sub-500ms voice cloning and synthesis.
* **Display Label**: **"Built with / Neural Voice Synthesis"**.
* **Application Link**: [elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io/) (Commercial partnership intake)
